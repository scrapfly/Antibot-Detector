Bulk scan

Scan a list of pages one at a time in a background tab. Each page is a real visit with your browser's cookies, and what it finds is added to History as usual.

One address per line. A sitemap .xml address adds the pages it lists. Duplicates are removed, and only http and https pages are scanned.

History is paused, so the pages are scanned but not recorded.
